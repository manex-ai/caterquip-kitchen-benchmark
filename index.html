BUYING WITH CONFIDENCE / 04

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Ask before purchasing equipment. This concept does not promise availability or approve engineering specifications.

Can I request a custom size?

The public listing describes custom manufacturing. Include desired dimensions and ask the supplier to confirm what can be made.

Should I choose gas or electricity?

Confirm the equipment specifications, electrical capacity, gas supply, ventilation and installation requirements with qualified professionals.

Can I order or pay here?

No. This independent design benchmark only prepares a message locally. It does not accept payments, place orders or transmit enquiries.

What is included in the price?

Request a written quotation specifying equipment, tax, delivery, installation, warranty and any additional costs.

What capacity options are listed?

The directory lists industrial stoves with 3–6 plates, fryers with 5, 10 and 20 litre bowls, and oil-jacketed pots in 90, 135, 150, 180 and 225 litre sizes. Ask whether your preferred configuration is currently offered.
